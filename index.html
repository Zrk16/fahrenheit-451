The World of F°451

Books burn at 451°F

In Bradbury's America, firemen don't put out fires — they start them. Every book is contraband. Every library is ash. The government discovered that an ignorant population is a content one, so they replaced literature with wall-sized television screens and outlawed independent thought entirely.

Citizens race through life at speed — never pausing, never questioning, never reading. Happiness is mandated. Curiosity is sedition. And if you hide a book, the mechanical hound will find you first.
